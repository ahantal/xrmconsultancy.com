Since 1999

The Internet Entrepreneur

Ali launched his first internet venture in 1999, at the beginning of the commercial internet era. Since then, he has founded and built technology companies across multiple technology cycles, gaining hands-on experience turning emerging technologies into real-world businesses.

His entrepreneurial journey includes co-founding Mezun.com, one of the early online platforms serving the global Turkish community, and later founding and leading ventures across immersive technologies, spatial computing, and AI.

Spatial computing since 2015

Building the Geospatial Foundation

In 2015, Ali founded VR Masters, initially focused on virtual reality. As the team expanded its R&D into augmented and mixed reality in 2017, the company's focus increasingly shifted toward connecting digital experiences with the physical world.

By 2018, the team was working with GIS, spatial computing, and Visual Positioning System (VPS) technologies, developing persistent and shared AR experiences and the foundations of a spatial content management platform. Reflecting this broader direction, VR Masters became XR Masters.

Since then, XR Masters has delivered more than 100 XR projects and experiences across the U.S., Europe, the Middle East, North Africa, and Central Asia. This work evolved into MyGeoVerse, bringing together geospatial content management, points of interest, spatial positioning, persistent and shared XR, and location-aware AI agents.

Today, the convergence of generative AI and spatial intelligence represents the next chapter of a technology journey XR Masters began more than a decade ago.

Open by design

Building an Open Spatial Web

Ali has long advocated for an open and interoperable Spatial Web, where spatial content, positioning, and experiences can work across platforms, devices, and ecosystems rather than being locked into proprietary silos.

Through his leadership and participation in the Open AR Cloud Association, OGC GeoPose, and the Metaverse Standards Forum, Ali and XR Masters have actively contributed to advancing open standards and interoperability for spatial computing.

This philosophy also shapes XRM Consultancy's approach: help organizations develop strategies and architectures that can evolve across technologies, devices, AI platforms, and ecosystems rather than becoming unnecessarily dependent on a single proprietary platform.

Today
