Who it's for

AI is moving from understanding information to understanding the physical world. Organizations that operate physical places, such as shopping malls, airports, cruise ship piers, stadiums, museums, cultural heritage sites, tourism destinations, campuses, and other high-traffic venues, will need to decide how their AI understands location, orientation, surroundings, and real-world context.

This includes organizations that haven't started building anything yet, as well as enterprises, public-sector organizations, and technology companies already developing AI, XR, or spatial products. XRM Consultancy helps figure out what to build, how to build it, and how to turn it into both a better visitor experience and a business opportunity, grounded in a working platform and the open standards that determine whether these experiences hold up over time.

Founder

Ali C. Hantal

Founder of XR Masters and Co-president at Open AR Cloud

An entrepreneur, strategic technology advisor, and spatial computing pioneer whose career has evolved alongside major shifts in computing, from the early commercial internet to immersive technologies, geospatial computing, and today's convergence of AI with the physical world.

He launched his first internet venture in 1999 and co-founded Mezun.com, one of the early online platforms serving the global Turkish community. In 2015 he founded VR Masters, later XR Masters, which has delivered persistent, shared XR experiences across more than 100 projects in the U.S., Europe, the Middle East, North Africa, and Central Asia, and brought that work together in MyGeoVerse.

Through the Open AR Cloud Association, OGC GeoPose, and the Metaverse Standards Forum, he has long advocated for an open, interoperable Spatial Web. He brings a founder's perspective, an operator's pragmatism, and a strategist's view of what is coming next to every engagement.
