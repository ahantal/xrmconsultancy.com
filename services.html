What we're known for

Location-Aware AI Agents

Strategy and design for AI agents that understand not just what someone is asking, but where they are and what's around them, for organizations exploring this for the first time through those ready to build and deploy.

We help organizations plan, design, and build AI agents that understand a person's location, orientation, surroundings, nearby places and objects, and physical context, not only their question. Depending on the environment and positioning technology, this can mean highly precise positioning, down to roughly 10 centimeters, rather than simple GPS-level location.

This work is for organizations at any stage, whether you're exploring where location-aware AI could create value, defining use cases and strategy, designing the experience, or ready to prototype, build, and deploy. You do not need an existing AI initiative or development team to start this conversation.

It draws directly on hands-on platform experience building MyGeoVerse, and on standards leadership as Chief Adoption Officer of the OGC's GeoPose working group. GeoPose is the standard that defines how location and orientation are represented across spatial computing platforms.

Think of the information kiosk, reimagined

Malls, airports, museums, and major venues have relied on the information kiosk for decades. Location-aware AI transforms that into a personalized, conversational assistant that travels with the visitor on their phone or AI glasses. Instead of walking to a desk, visitors can simply ask:

  • "Where should I eat?"
  • "Where is my gate?"
  • "What is worth seeing near me?"
  • "Take me to this store."
  • "What am I looking at?"
  • "What should I do next?"

The AI understands the visitor's physical context and responds with relevant information, recommendations, and spatial guidance.

Built for high-traffic venues

Shopping malls, airports, cruise ship piers, stadiums, museums, tourism destinations, and cultural heritage sites will increasingly need their own location-aware AI agents to assist visitors. These agents become a new interface between a physical destination and the people moving through it. We help organizations prepare for this transition before they necessarily know how to implement it themselves.

Real-time venue knowledge

The venue or operator can control and update the information available to the AI: points of interest, stores and restaurants, events, exhibits, services, offers, recommendations, temporary and operational information, and sponsored or promoted locations, all in real time. Rather than relying on generic information from the internet, the AI draws on venue-controlled, current, and contextually relevant knowledge.

A new revenue channel

Location-aware AI is not only a customer-service or navigation tool. It can create a new revenue channel for physical venues. When a visitor asks where to have lunch, what to do next, or where to find a good coffee shop, the AI can offer useful recommendations that also incorporate promoted destinations, sponsored recommendations, special offers, and paid visibility, the same way digital platforms monetize recommendations and discovery, applied to the physical world.

Better visitor experience, better discovery, and a new revenue channel for the venue: the AI's primary purpose remains helping the visitor; monetization comes from recommendations that are genuinely useful.

Serves: shopping malls, airports, cruise ship piers, stadiums, museums, tourism destinations, campuses, and other high-traffic venues, along with enterprises, public-sector organizations, and technology companies developing AI, XR, or spatial products
Typical engagement: technology assessment, strategy engagement, fixed-scope project, or ongoing advisory

What we're known for

Persistent & Shared XR Experiences

Design and advisory for multi-user, persistent XR: spatial experiences that stay put, stay in sync across users, and stay interoperable across devices and platforms.

We help organizations design persistent, multi-user XR experiences across tourism, cultural heritage, museums, retail, entertainment, education, campuses, events, branded experiences, navigation, and location-based storytelling, built correctly the first time, rather than locked into a single vendor's proprietary stack.

As with location-aware AI, this work can start at any stage, from early idea and opportunity discovery, through strategy, experience design, and technology selection, to prototyping and deployment. You don't need an existing XR initiative to start this conversation.

This work is grounded in the open protocols developed through Open AR Cloud, where we serve as Co-president, and the Metaverse Standards Forum, keeping experiences interoperable as platforms and devices evolve.

Our work can include spatial experience design, multi-user architecture planning, interoperability and open-standards guidance, and testbed design and implementation.

Serves: tourism, cultural heritage, museums, retail, entertainment, education, campuses, and organizations building persistent XR experiences
Typical engagement: fixed-scope project, testbed engagement, or ongoing advisory
